Lemma A.3 Assume that ∃0 < c < 1/(2γ) such that for all a ∈A and all p ∈[−γ, 1 + γ],
                          |M(p|a) −M(α∗a|a)| ≥c|p −α∗a|.
Then, for all a ∈A, k ∈{0, 1, 2, . . . }, and T ∈N
E[(α1 −α∗A1)2|A1+k = a]
 ≤(1 −2cγ)T −1E[(α1 −α∗A1)2|AT +k = a]
    T
+ X(1 −2cγ)T −t  γ2 + 2(1 + γ)E[|α∗At−1 −α∗At||AT +k = a] + E[(α∗At−1 −α∗At)2|AT +k = a]   .
   t=2

Furthermore, if we assume that ∀a ∈A and k ∈{0, 1, 2, . . . },
         E[|α∗At−k −α∗At−k−1||At = a] ≤ϵ1 and E[(α∗At−k −α∗At−k−1)2|At = a] ≤ϵ2,

then we ﬁnd that

                                         1
                 E[(α1 −α∗A1)2|A1+k = a] ≤     γ2 + 2(1 + γ)ϵ1 + ϵ2  .                                          2cγ

Proof:  Fix any T ∈N. Since {(αt, At)}t∈N is stationary we have that
                     E[(α1 −α∗A1)2|A1+k] = E[(αT −α∗AT )2|AT +k].
Now note that
E[(αT −α∗AT )2|AT +k] = E[(αT −α∗AT −1)2|AT +k] + 2E[(αT −α∗AT −1)(α∗AT −1 −α∗AT )|AT +k]
              + E[(α∗AT −1 −α∗AT )2|AT +k]
                 ≤E[(αT −α∗AT −1)2|AT +k] + 2(1 + γ)E[|α∗AT −1 −α∗AT ||AT +k]
              + E[(α∗AT −1 −α∗AT )2|AT +k],

where on the last line we have applied Lemma 4.1. The ﬁrst term above can be bounded as
      E[(αT −α∗AT −1)2|AT +k]
      ≤E[(αT −1 −α∗AT −1)2|AT +k] + 2E[γ(α −errT −1)(αT −1 −α∗AT −1)|AT +k] + γ2,

where we additionally have that
            E[(α −errT )(αT −1 −α∗AT −1)|AT +k]
        = E[(α −E[errT −1|AT −1, αT −1, AT +k])(αT −1 −α∗AT −1)|AT +k]
        = E[(M(α∗AT −1|AT1) −M(αT −1|AT −1))(αT −1 −α∗AT −1)|AT +k]
           ≤−cE[(αT −1 −α∗AT −1)2|AT +k].

Whence,
          E[(αT −α∗AT −1)2|AT +k] ≤(1 −2cγ)E[(αT −1 −α∗AT −1)2|AT +k] + γ2,

and plugging this into our ﬁrst inequality yields
  E[(αT −α∗AT )2|AT +k] ≤(1 −2cγ)E[(αT −1 −α∗AT −1)2|AT +k] + γ2
               + 2(1 + γ)E[|α∗AT −1 −α∗AT ||AT +k] + E[(α∗AT −1 −α∗AT )2|AT +k].

Repeating this argument inductively gives
E[(αT −α∗AT )2|AT +k = a]

    T
+ X(1 −2cγ)T −t  γ2 + 2(1 + γ)E[|α∗At−1 −α∗At||AT +k = a] + E[(α∗At−1 −α∗At)2|AT +k = a]   .
   t=2
The ﬁnal part of the lemma follows by sending T →∞.                     □
