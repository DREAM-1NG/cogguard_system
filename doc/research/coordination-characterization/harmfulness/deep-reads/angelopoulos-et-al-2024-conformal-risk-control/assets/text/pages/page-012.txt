Conformal prediction can be thought of as controlling the expectation of an indicator loss function.
Recall that the risk upper bound (2) specializes to the conformal coverage guarantee in (1) when
the loss function is the indicator of a miscoverage event. The conformal risk control procedure
specializes to conformal prediction under this loss function as well. However, the risk lower bound

these correspondences.
