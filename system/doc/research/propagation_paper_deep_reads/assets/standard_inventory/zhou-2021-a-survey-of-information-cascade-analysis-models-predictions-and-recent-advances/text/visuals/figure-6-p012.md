## Body references

- [PDF p.11] 3.1.4 Evolving Trends. Characterizing the evolving trends of information cascades has been shown to yield informative signals for popularity prediction [8, 74, 116, 224]. Such temporal pat- terns of time series can be categorized into several types, e.g., smoothly increasing or suddenly bursting/decaying, dependent on different clustering algorithms. In Figure 6, we show 10 evolving trends of APS paper citations spanning 20 years by applying an agglomerative hierarchical clus- tering algorithm [75]. We can see that most of the clusters follow the trend that paper receives most of its citations in the first few years and then quickly diminishes in the following years.
