As for the regression task, mean-square error (MSE) and its variants are the most popular met-
rics. Popularity is often shown in logarithmic scale, e.g., MSLE or RMSLE, to prevent the loss
functions or metrics being affected by extreme values and assure numerical stability [29, 38]. Co-
efficient of determination/correlation and their variants, rankings [184], k-top coverage [150, 246],
are also frequently used metrics in some specific scenarios.
The choice of a metric is often subjective, even under a well-defined problem formulation. Pre-
vious works found that a model might perform well in one metric but significantly drop in an-
other [72], making it difficult to do a fair comparison between various approaches. We summarize
frequently used metrics as well as their adopters in Table 1.

patterns in network, analyzing time-series evolution trends, and building prediction models.
