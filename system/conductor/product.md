# Product

CogGuard is an event-monitoring workbench for analysts who need to inspect collected social-media evidence, propagation behavior, coordination signals, and review cases.

Propagation monitoring presents observed facts separately from forecasts. Its role analysis must help analysts find evidence-backed accounts and paths without presenting inferred relationships as confirmed causality.

Implemented analyst workspaces are: dashboard, data acquisition, coordination discovery and detection, propagation monitoring, account profiling, event review with semantic evidence, and system operations. Event review keeps Student assessment and Teacher/MARO advisory separate from an analyst-confirmed decision; system-generated countermeasure content remains a draft for human review.
